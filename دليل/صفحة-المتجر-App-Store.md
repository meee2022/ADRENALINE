أدرينالين للوجبات الصحية — مطبخك الصحي اليومي في قطر.

اختر خطتك (تنشيف، لياقة، تضخيم أو خطة مخصّصة)، وحدّد عدد الوجبات والسناك يومياً، ونحن نطبخ لك صباح كل يوم ونوصّل لك بلا رسوم توصيل.

Fresh, calorie-counted meals cooked every morning and delivered to your door. Pick your meals from the menu or let the smart plan choose for you.

**Description:**
Adrenaline Healthy Food — your daily healthy kitchen in Qatar.

Choose your plan (Diet, Fitness, Bulk or a custom plan), set how many meals and snacks you want per day, and we cook for you every morning and deliver for free.

What you can do in the app:
• Browse the full menu: 200+ dishes with real photos and calories, protein, carbs and fat for each one.
• Explore plans, packages and prices, and contact us directly to subscribe.
• Subscribers: pick your meals for every day of your subscription from the weekly kitchen menu, or let the smart plan suggest a full week that fits your goal and dietary restrictions.
• Track your daily plan and delivery dates, and swap any meal before it is prepared.
• Calorie calculator: find your daily calories and macros for your goal.
• Allergy and restriction alerts before you pick a meal.

All meals are prepared under nutritionist supervision with fresh ingredients, and menus rotate through the month.

Subscriptions are arranged with our team (WhatsApp or phone) and activated on your account. No payments are made inside the app.
