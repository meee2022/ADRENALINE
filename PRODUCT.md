# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operations staff at Adrenaline Healthy Food in Qatar, including administrators, kitchen staff, delivery teams, nutritionists, and inventory managers. Staff use the system throughout a busy working day to manage meal subscriptions, production, preparation, delivery, and customer requirements in Arabic and English.

## Product Purpose

Adrenaline Meals Manager coordinates the daily meal-subscription workflow from customer plans through kitchen preparation and delivery. Success means staff can find the next action quickly, handle large daily volumes accurately, and preserve dietary restrictions and customer-specific instructions without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Clear, energetic, and trustworthy. The product should feel operationally calm under pressure, premium without decoration, and welcoming without becoming playful.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense spreadsheets disguised as interfaces, oversized dashboard decoration, nested cards, low-contrast controls, repeated visual noise, and layouts that require excessive scrolling or scanning. Operational warnings must remain visible without making every item feel urgent.

## Design Principles

1. Prioritize the next operational action over decoration.
2. Make large datasets easy to scan, filter, and compare.
3. Use hierarchy and progressive detail to reduce cognitive load.
4. Preserve dietary warnings and status information without ambiguity.
5. Support Arabic and English equally across desktop and mobile layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, minimum 40px touch targets, reduced-motion compatibility, and redundant icon-plus-text treatment for warnings. Layout and typography must remain usable in both RTL and LTR directions.
